### 🌐 Key Features Available to Users
1. **Bilingual Interface:** Switch between English and हिंदी with a single click in the sidebar.
2. **Camera & Upload Support:** Real-time mobile camera capture or multi-file upload.
3. **Interactive Dark GIS Map:** Live Folium OpenStreetMap layer with color-coded severity markers.
4. **Official PDF Work Orders:** Downloadable municipal compliance work orders with photographic proof and audit signatures.
